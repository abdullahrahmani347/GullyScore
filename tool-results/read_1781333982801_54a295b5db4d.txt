     6→     6→     6→## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
     7→     7→     7→
     8→     8→     8→GullyScore is a mobile-first progressive web application for tracking informal cricket matches — gully games, colony tournaments, university grounds, terrace cricket — at a level of fidelity that paper scorecards and WhatsApp groups cannot match.
     9→     9→     9→
    10→    10→    10→**Core thesis:** Every serious gully match eventually produces a score dispute. The current tools are either too casual (manually typing into a group chat) or too complex (full-blown sports management platforms). GullyScore is purpose-built for the middle — fast to start, cricket-accurate in logic, beautiful on a phone screen, and persistent across sessions.
    11→    11→    11→
    12→    12→    12→**Target geography:** South Asia (India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh) and diaspora communities globally. The app should feel culturally native — names, examples, and aesthetic sensibility should match this context.

    16→    16→    16→## 2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
    17→    17→    17→
    18→    18→    18→| Current Solution | Critical Failures |
    19→    19→    19→|-----------------|-------------------|
    20→    20→    20→| Paper scorecard | Disputes, illegible handwriting, water/weather damage, no searchable history, cannot be shared digitally |
    21→    21→    21→| WhatsApp manual updates | Unstructured, tedious, prone to miscounts, no ball-by-ball granularity |
    22→    22→    22→| ESPNcricinfo/Cricbuzz apps | Require registration, designed for broadcast cricket not informal games, overkill UI |
    23→    23→    23→| Generic sports trackers | No cricket-specific scoring rules (extras, strike rotation, over completion) |
    24→    24→    24→| Excel spreadsheets | Completely unusable on mobile, requires expertise to build |
    25→    25→    25→
    26→    26→    26→GullyScore eliminates all five failure modes simultaneously.

   346→   346→   346→#### 6.4.5 Scoring Logic (Canonical)
   347→   347→   347→
   348→   348→   348→```
   349→   349→   349→DEFINITIONS:
   350→   350→   350→  batsmanRuns  = runs credited to striker (0 for byes/leg-byes)
   351→   351→   351→  extraRuns    = runs to extras column (1 for wide/NB penalty + any bye/LB runs)
   352→   352→   352→  totalRuns    = batsmanRuns + extraRuns
   353→   353→   353→  isLegal      = delivery counts toward the 6-ball over (NOT wide, NOT no-ball)
   354→   354→   354→
   355→   355→   355→BALL RECORDING SEQUENCE:
   356→   356→   356→  1. Insert Ball record (persist immediately)
   357→   357→   357→  2. Update BatsmanInnings (striker):
   358→   358→   358→       balls += (isWide ? 0 : 1)
   359→   359→   359→       runs += batsmanRuns
   360→   360→   360→       fours += (batsmanRuns == 4 ? 1 : 0)
   361→   361→   361→       sixes += (batsmanRuns == 6 ? 1 : 0)
   362→   362→   362→       If isWicket AND dismissedPlayer == striker:
   363→   363→   363→         isOut = true, set dismissalType, bowler, fielder
   364→   364→   364→  3. If RUN_OUT of non-striker: update non-striker BatsmanInnings similarly
   365→   365→   365→  4. Update BowlerInnings (current bowler):

   372→   372→   372→  5. Update Innings:

   382→   382→   382→  6. Strike rotation:

   387→   387→   387→  7. Check innings completion:
